SHIELD FACINGS & FIRING ARCS

Like in Star Fleet Battles and Starfleet Command, your deflector grid is split into four facings: fore, aft, port and starboard. Incoming fire drains only the facing it strikes. Turn to present a fresh shield and let damaged facings regenerate. Each phaser bank, pulse cannon, and torpedo launcher checks its firing arc before firing.

FLEET CLASSES & SPECIAL TACTICS

Frigates & Escorts (Freedom, Nova, Defiant) use speed and agility to outmaneuver capital ships. Destroyers & Medium Cruisers (Miranda, Akira, Steamrunner, Excelsior, Constitution) balance hull strength with heavy torpedo pods. Heavy Explorers (Intrepid, Galaxy, Sovereign, Prometheus) command wide phaser arcs—and the Prometheus Class can press SPECIAL (G) to engage Multi-Vector Assault Mode (MVAM), splitting into Alpha, Beta, and Gamma combat vectors! Battlecruisers & Dreadnoughts (Kirov, Inquiry) unleash multi-torpedo salvos and combined phaser array + pulse cannon barrages.
